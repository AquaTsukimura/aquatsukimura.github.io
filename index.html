Bound by Duty, Connected by Love

In a world that demanded they become weapons, Ali Maud and Lizzy Seiran found their salvation in each other. Behind the cold walls of the orphanage, far away from the front lines, they carved out a fragile sanctuary.

Publicly walking side-by-side, secretly sharing quiet moments and intertwined hands, their relationship was a testament to humanity amidst the horrors of war. A love story wrapped in everyday warmth, yet haunted by an inevitable tragedy.

Ali Maud

A gentle heart forced to endure the unbearable weight of waiting. Ali wished nothing more than for Seiran to stay safely by her side. Recognizing Seiran's unyielding sense of justice, Ali supported her quietly, pouring her hopes and devotions into a single glass bead—a prayer for a safe return that would ultimately become her only remnant.

Lizzy Seiran

To the orphanage, she was a dedicated soldier fulfilling her debt; to Ali, she was a girl who secretly cherished makeup, jewelry, and soft moments in the dark. Driven by loyalty and an innate desire to protect, Seiran faced the battlefield with fierce resolve. Her deepest tragedy wasn't the war itself, but the final, desperate yearning to simply return to Ali's side.

A Prayer in Glass

Knowing Seiran's secret affection for beautiful things, Ali crafted a necklace from a glass bead, tucking a heartfelt prayer inside. Seiran wore it onto the battlefield, touching it to ground herself in thoughts of home. When the war finally took her, it left behind nothing but ashes and the untouched glass bead—a devastating symbol of a love that outlasted life itself.

Ali × Lizzy

A beautiful love eclipsed by tragedy. May their memories remain eternal.
