08 / and now for the real important thing

Ali × Lizzy

okay yeah, but let's talk about the part i absolutely will not shut up about. the official character material describes Ali as someone who gently stays close to Seiran, while Lizzy is the more justice-driven, awkwardly caring half of the pair. the result is a relationship space that feels tender, tense, and painfully suited to KimiShinu's world.

Lizzy Seiran

principled / caring / beautifully awkward
